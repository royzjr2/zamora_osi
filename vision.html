How I Would Build This Role
A vision for enterprise strategy, organizational health, and performance measurement at Irving ISD
The first 12 months
The thesis
Districts rarely fail for lack of information. They fail because no one is accountable for how the information fits together: where enrollment is heading, whether capacity matches it, whether staffing follows, whether compensation rewards what we say matters, and whether any of it is actually being implemented as designed.
This role exists to hold that relationship. Not to produce more reports, but to make the connections visible and consequential.
How I would approach the work
01 · Strategic Planning & Execution
Make the strategic plan operational, not ceremonial
Translate priorities into named outcomes, measures, owners, milestones, and review cycles. Establish an enterprise review cadence that surfaces barriers, dependencies, and required decisions before they become crises, and reports honestly when something is off track.
02 · Portfolio & Capacity
Treat enrollment, facilities, and staffing as one system
Enrollment forecasting, planning denominators, capacity reconciliation, and staffing allotment modeling are the same problem viewed from different angles. An annual utilization review reported whether or not anything is wrong turns a decade-scale risk into a governed cycle rather than a periodic crisis.
03 · Organizational Health
Diagnose the organization the way we diagnose student learning
Build a repeatable diagnostic covering culture, engagement, trust, role clarity, and decision rights. Instrument design is a technical discipline. The value is not in surveying more, it is in asking questions that produce action and closing the loop visibly so people keep answering.
04 · Strategic Compensation
Protect a live system through implementation
Strategic Compensation and Irving Measures of Excellence live or die on whether teachers believe the rules are clear and consistently applied. Documented business rules, validated data sources, defensible calculations, and communication people can actually follow. Cross-functional governance with HR, Finance, Legal, and Academics.
05 · Campus Capacity
Make organizational readiness visible alongside outcomes
A validated 0 to 100 measure of campus organizational capacity, built from data Texas districts already report and already computed for every Irving campus across seven years. It answers a question ratings cannot: whether a campus is built to keep performing, or is carrying a result it cannot reproduce.
06 · District Effectiveness Model
Close the gap between how campuses and central office are measured
Campuses are measured continuously and on outcomes. Central office is measured largely on role execution. Closing that asymmetry is a credibility question before it is a governance one, and it starts by scoring this function first, on the draft instrument, before asking any other division to adopt it.
Why there is no seventh domain

Leadership capacity is what these six produce together, not a separate workstream. Principal tenure and stability are measured in campus capacity. Appraisal and effectiveness levels sit in strategic compensation. Multi rater feedback and competency frameworks live in organizational health. Succession is a continuity risk named in planning, and consolidation forces placement decisions in portfolio work. Assembled, they answer who is ready, where the bench is thin, and which campuses are about to lose the leadership their results depend on. That is a synthesis question, which is why it belongs to the function rather than to any one domain.

First twelve months
Days 1–90
Listen, inventory, establish truth
Meet every Cabinet member and division leader. Inventory what data and diagnostics already exist. Reconcile enrollment and capacity figures. Understand where Strategic Compensation is genuinely working and where trust is thin. Publish nothing until the numbers are defensible.
Months 4–8
Build the instruments
Stand up the first organizational health diagnostic. Draft executive scorecards with the Superintendent and Cabinet. Establish the enterprise review cadence. Define the leadership competency framework. Bring the first capacity and enrollment picture to the table.
Months 9–12
Make it consequential
First full review cycle completed. First scorecard reported to the Board. Strategic Compensation cycle executed with documented, auditable rules. Recommendations delivered on what to continue, refine, expand, or stop, with evidence behind each one.
How I would operate
Lead through influence
Division leaders retain operational ownership. My leverage is clarity, evidence, and follow-through, not authority over their teams.
Publish on a calendar
Scorecards, reviews, and reports on fixed dates. Predictability is what makes measurement feel like governance rather than surveillance.
Say the inconvenient thing
The value of this seat is unfiltered judgment. A Superintendent advisor who only confirms what is already believed is not worth the position.
Close every loop
Nobody answers a second survey if the first one changed nothing. Visible response is the precondition for honest data.
Enterprise strategy is not a document. It is whether the organization can see itself clearly enough to adjust before it has to react.
